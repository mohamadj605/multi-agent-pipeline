## Recommended Laser Settings (starting points)

These are starting points for **3 mm basswood/birch plywood**. Every machine, tube/diode and material batch is different, so run a test on scrap first.

| Operation | Color | 40W CO2 | 60W CO2 | 10-20W Diode |
|-----------|-------|---------|---------|--------------|
| **Cut** | Red | 100% power, ~10 mm/s | 80% power, ~15 mm/s | Full power, ~3-5 mm/s, 2-4 passes |
| **Score** | Blue | 15-20% power, ~300 mm/s | 12-15% power, ~350 mm/s | 20-30% power, ~1000 mm/min |
| **Engrave** | Black | 20-30% power, ~250 mm/s | 18-25% power, ~300 mm/s | 40-60% power, ~1500 mm/min |

**Recommended order of operations:** Engrave (black), then Score (blue), then Cut (red) last. This keeps the material from shifting before the engraving is done.

**Tips**
- Because the black layer is vector outlines, it may run as a line engrave or score. Fill it in your software if you want solid lettering.
- Use masking tape or transfer tape on the surface to reduce smoke stains.
- Turn on air assist. Wipe edges with a damp cloth or fine sandpaper after cutting.
- Check that your software imports at 150 mm. If the size is off, scale to 150 mm width.
